Dog Encyclopedia

Every picture here is a simple drawing made for this project, not a photograph.

Beagle

Size
Small to medium, about 33–41 cm tall and 9–11 kg.
Personality
Curious, cheerful and very social. Happiest with company and a job to do.
Everyday care
Two walks a day plus sniffing time. Brush once a week and check those long ears.

Golden Retriever

Size
Large, about 51–61 cm tall and 25–34 kg.
Personality
Gentle, patient and eager to please. Friendly with people, children and other dogs.
Everyday care
Long daily exercise and brushing three times a week to control shedding.

Poodle

Size
Toy to standard, about 24–60 cm tall depending on the variety.
Personality
Clever, alert and playful. Learns quickly and likes being close to its owner.
Everyday care
Coat needs combing every other day and a trim every few weeks. Short walks plus play.
